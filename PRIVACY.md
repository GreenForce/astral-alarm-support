# Privacy Policy for Astral Alarm: REM Wake

Effective date: October 9, 2026

Astral Alarm: REM Wake ("the app") is an Apple Watch app that estimates when you enter REM sleep and wakes you at that moment. This policy explains what information the app uses and what happens to it.

## Summary

The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any of your data. Everything it uses stays on your Apple Watch. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party services.

## Information the app uses

With your permission, the app reads the following on your Apple Watch:

- **Heart rate**, while a sleep session is running, to estimate your sleep stage.
- **Wrist movement** from the watch's motion sensor, while a sleep session is running, for the same purpose.
- **Past sleep stages and the heart rate recorded during them**, from Apple Health for the last 14 days, to learn your typical heart rate in REM sleep.

The app also stores information you create in it:

- Your settings (wake window, sensitivity, alarm options).
- A record of each night for up to 30 days: start and wake-up time, the estimated sleep stages, how the alarm ended, and the result percentage you choose to enter.

## How the information is used

The information is used only inside the app, on your watch, to detect REM sleep, sound the alarm and show you your own results. It is not used for advertising, marketing or profiling.

## Where the information is kept

All of it stays on your Apple Watch. The app has no server and makes no network connections. Nothing is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

The app starts a workout session overnight so that the watch keeps measuring your heart rate. The app does not save that workout to Apple Health. Heart rate readings taken by the watch itself are stored in Apple Health by watchOS, as with any workout, and are governed by Apple's privacy policy.

## Retention and deletion

- Night records are deleted automatically after 30 days.
- Deleting the app from your Apple Watch deletes all of its settings and records.
- You can withdraw Health access at any time on the watch in Settings > Health > Apps > Astral Alarm.

## Notifications

The app schedules a local notification on your watch as a backup alarm. It is created and delivered on the device; no push notification service is involved.

## Age

The app is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is rated 18+ on the App Store. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Not a medical device

Sleep stages shown by the app are estimates based on heart rate and movement. The app is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat or prevent any condition.
